Making an omelette

Ingredients:

  • 3-4 eggs
  • Chopped bell peppers
  • Chopped onions
  • About half a spoon of salt
  • About half a spoon of red chili powder

Steps to Cook

  1. Crack the eggs into a tall glass or bowl.
  2. Add in the chopped vegetables.
  3. Add in your spices.
  4. Stir until your batter is blended.
