Making an omelette

Ingredients:

  • 3-4 eggs
  • Chopped bell peppers
  • Chopped onions
  • About half a spoon of salt
  • About half a spoon of red chili powder

Steps to Cook

  1. Crack the eggs into a tall glass or bowl
  2. Add in the chopped vegetables
  3. Add in your spices
  4. Stir until your batter is blended
  5. Keeping an eye on your omelette, toast two slices of bread, lightly coating the pan with butter first.
  6. If you want two omelettes, pour half of your batter on the pan, but if you want 3 omelettes, pour 1/3 of the batter.
  7. When the sides of the omelette become thin, almost papery, and you can lift the sides without breaking the omelette, flip the omelette.
  8. Place the omelette between the two slices of bread and add your preferred items in the sandwich (avacado, onion, etc.).
  9. Serve and enjoy!
