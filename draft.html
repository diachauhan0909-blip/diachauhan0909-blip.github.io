Making an omelette

Ingredients:

  • 3-4 eggs
  • Chopped bell peppers
  • Chopped onions
  • About half a spoon of salt
  • About half a spoon of red chili powder

Steps to Cook

  1. Crack the eggs into a tall glass or bowl
  2. Add in the chopped vegetables
  3. Add in your spices
  4. Stir until your batter is blended
  5. If you want to make two eggs pour about half of your batter onto the pan. If you want to make three omelettes, pour about 1/3 of the batter onto the pan.
